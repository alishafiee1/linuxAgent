# Plan before building

Skip this for small, clear tasks.

1. **Understand.** Look at what exists first. Restate the goal in two sentences.
2. **Ask** with `ask_user` only what changes the result, all at once.
3. **Compare** two or three approaches when they differ, with their cost, and recommend one.
4. **Slice** the work into steps that each leave something working and checkable. Say how each will be checked.
5. **Cut.** Remove what the goal does not need.
6. **Build** step by step, and check each one before the next. If the plan turns out wrong, say so and change it.
